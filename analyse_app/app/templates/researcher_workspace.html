Accepted keys: cdr_ids, demographics (age_min, age_max, sex, region), conditions, medications, type_canonical. An empty cdr_ids means every source.

Defining a cohort resolves it across the sources and applies the research-consent filter. Counts below k are suppressed when the cohort is analysed, not here.

What this workspace can do

Seven analyses are available on the analysis page: describe, histogram, frequency, correlation, compare groups, over time, completeness. The method for a correlation is derived from the data, never chosen — picking between Pearson and Spearman is a statistics question, and asking it of a clinician is how the wrong one gets picked.

Every result passes k-anonymity twice: once at each source before anything leaves it, and once after merging — because two safe partials can merge into an unsafe total.
