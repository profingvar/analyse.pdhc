## NOT FULLY DEPLOYED — linear_regression and kaplan_meier
Both are implemented, tested and in the engine REGISTRY, and **no spec can
request either**: they are absent from the spec's `Analysis` union. Markers sit
in `app/engine/__init__.py` (the REGISTRY) and `app/engine/regression.py` so
they are found by reading, not by another dead-code sweep. Exposing them needs
decisions nobody has taken — what a linear regression declares as predictors vs
outcome, how a survival spec expresses its event and censoring. **#712 should
not stand up nodes before someone takes them.**

## Read #711 before rebuilding ANY pdhc service
SQLAlchemy 2.1 changed the default driver for a bare `postgresql://` URL from
psycopg2 to psycopg v3. 14 of 21 running services still use a bare URL and will
crash-loop on their next cache-missing rebuild — sso.pdhc among them. analyse
is fixed (pinned <2.1 AND an explicit `+psycopg2` URL); the rest are not.

Unit tests cannot see this: analyse had 467 passing tests while the deploy
crash-looped, because the local venv still held 2.0.51.
